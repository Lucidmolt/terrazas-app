# Terrazas.app - Provider Compliance Standards

## 1. Licensing Requirements
To maintain "Verified Pro" status, providers must meet the following state-specific criteria:

### **Texas (TX)**
- **General Landscaping**: No state-level "landscaping" license, but local municipal registration may apply.
- **Irrigation**: Must hold a **Licensed Irrigator** (LI) license from the **TCEQ**.
  - *Verification*: [TCEQ Search](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/licensing/verify.html)
- **Pest Control/Weed Control**: Must hold a **Commercial Applicator** license from the **TDA**.
  - *Verification*: [TDA Search](https://data.texas.gov/dataset/Texas-Department-of-Agriculture-Pesticide-License-D/)
- **Business**: Must provide a valid EIN/Business Registration.

### **California (CA)**
- **C-27 Landscaping Contractor License**: Required for any project exceeding $500.
  - *Verification*: [CSLB License Search](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/OnlineServices/CheckLicenseII/CheckLicense.aspx)
- **Business License**: Valid business tax certificate for the primary city of operation.

## 2. Insurance (COI)
Providers MUST upload a Certificate of Insurance (COI) with the following minimums:
- **General Liability**: $1,000,000 per occurrence.
- **Additionally Insured**: *Terrazas.app* must be named as an additional insured party.

## 3. Readiness & Verification
- **Readiness Switch**: Providers must toggle "Available" in-app to receive broadcast jobs.
- **Geo-Verification**: App pings location during job arrival and completion.
- **Photo Evidence**: Mandatory "Before" and "After" photos uploaded to Supabase for every job.
- **Rating Threshold**: Pro status is suspended if rating falls below 4.2 stars.

## 4. Background Checks
- Mandatory criminal background check for all providers with property access.
- Integrated via **Sterling** or **Checkr**.
